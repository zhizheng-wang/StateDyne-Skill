# StateDyne 11.3 — real-model quality acceptance (not yet performed)

Passing structural tests is not evidence of model superiority. A claim of
"world-class" must survive matched, **blind**, multi-domain empirical comparison.

## Matched evaluation protocol

Compare (A) the same strongest available model with no skill but a normal user
request, (B) StateDyne 11.2.1, (C) StateDyne 11.3.0. Record exact model,
thinking level, tools, retrieval access, user facts, monetary cost, number of
real independent workers, and final artifact. Give all three equivalent task
and tool budgets; do not compare a cheap baseline to a high-compute skill and
claim the skill alone created the difference. Include a separate cost-adjusted
comparison. Blind graders to run identity and author preference.

## Tasks and negative controls

- Personal, incomplete-data explanation: distinguish observed phenomena from
  plausible generating mechanisms, make a **first-turn unreported prediction**,
  avoid fabricating memories; provide safe, usable advice when asked.
- Creator funding and distribution: compare direct B2B services, platform/
  channel-mediated uptake, pre-revenue funding and protected employment under
  identical constraints; explain who actually pays, adopts and captures value.
- Organizational/technical adoption: quantitative pilot improvement but low
  purchase conversion; independently discover environmental, selection and
  procurement barriers without inventing secret rules.
- Public and scientific research: actual primary sources, measures, relevant
  denominators, counterfactual net increment, viable interventions.
- Complex point/action combinations: explain mechanisms and when combining
  points beats a single point, with a genuine no-action baseline.
- Multi-turn: interruption, isolated new task, decline to provide more private
  context, and later "find a better path" without repeating earlier answers.
- Negative controls: simple conversion, familiar solved problem, and false
  mystery with no real hidden cause. No automatic "novelty" credit.

## Grading dimensions

Judge factual correctness separately from explanatory narrative. Strong output
must demonstrate: (1) accurate user goal and boundaries, (2) realistic causal
process with testable differentiators, (3) verified/unverified separation,
(4) newly generated, nontrivial consequences known **before** user follow-up,
(5) meaningful environmental/selection search, (6) structural diversity of
feasible paths rather than relabelled options, (7) concrete changed decision
or intervention, (8) useful continuation on demand, and (9) acceptable cost
and interaction burden. A confident incorrect invention is penalized heavily.

Keep independent evidence and timestamps showing what the user knew, what the
model newly predicted, and what happened later. Do not infer one version's
superiority from a small curated sample; report raw task-level wins, ties,
losses, grader disagreements and confidence intervals.
